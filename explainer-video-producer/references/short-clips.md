# Standalone short clips

Read when repurposing a finished explainer. Select a useful idea first, then choose its duration and framing. A request for “one-minute videos” normally means concise standalone pieces, not arbitrary sixty-second slices.

## Select complete ideas

Inspect the final source and accepted transcript. For each candidate, record the source ranges, complete opening thought, one useful claim or proof, satisfying endpoint and any dependency the viewer would otherwise be missing. Prefer distinct lessons over several clips repeating the same point.

Keep complete sentences where possible. A clean internal cut can remove a detour, provided it preserves the meaning and causal order. An authored hook title can orient the viewer; it cannot claim something the clipped evidence does not establish. Use the source as content evidence, not as a portrait-reference library.

Retain original audio, avatar and media that remain valid. A new caption or layout is not a reason to regenerate the creator's approved voice. If a clip genuinely needs new speech, identify that passage and use the accepted voice workflow before driving new lips.

## Frame the evidence

Use the requested destination geometry and its current supported format. A portrait export often needs a designed layout: an inspected software crop above and the presenter below, or a diagram with the presenter in a separate safe region. Do not blindly center-crop a landscape demonstration.

Keep the control, input, output and verification needed for the claim readable at phone size. If a dense screen cannot fit, choose a clearer source range or simplify the explanatory framing without reconstructing a fake UI. Avoid cropping away the condition that changes the meaning of a result.

Captions should remain readable and clear of eyes, mouth, important controls and platform overlays. Use exact word timing only when it exists. Otherwise use honest sentence/phrase blocks tied to measured speech boundaries; do not invent word-by-word alignment. Keep subtitle-free versions when the brief requests them.

Create a separate representative cover when the provider requires it. A face-free title/diagram treatment can communicate the clip's idea without generating a new portrait. Check cover/media dimension and size requirements from the current provider schema.

## Check the final clip

Verify each encoded file's expected geometry, duration, audio, full decode, caption legibility and actual loudness/true peak where measured. Inspect the hook, midpoint, important proof and ending, plus motion around any avatar/source join. The shortened version needs its own content check even when the full film passed.

Use a local manifest containing:

- Clip ID, title, one-sentence value and chosen destinations.
- Source master hash and ordered source time/sample/frame ranges.
- Actual final duration, geometry and media/cover/caption hashes.
- Final technical and sampled audiovisual review results and limitations.
- Intended account-specific copy, publication/schedule state and remote IDs.

## Copy and destination fit

Lead with the idea and practical value. Avoid generic promotion such as “in this six-minute video.” Titles, descriptions, alt text and captions should accurately name the subject without repetitive keyword or hashtag quotas.

| Destination | Suitable adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| YouTube Shorts | A standalone vertical idea with a specific title, concise description and useful link to the full source/guide where supported. |
| TikTok | Native short-form explanation with readable captions; use the true direct-publish method and applicable AI-content disclosure. |
| Pinterest | An eligible Video Pin, separate cover where required, descriptive title, relevant board and link to the canonical guide. |
| DEV | A substantive technical article with an appropriate video embed or link; a raw video post is not assumed supported. |
| LinkedIn / X | A complete useful point in the main post, with links placed according to the creator's policy and actual provider support. |
| Dribbble | Only original or authorized UI/UX/design work represented as an eligible shot or case study. Show the creator's actual contribution; do not claim someone else's app as portfolio work. |

Read [publishing-and-scheduling.md](publishing-and-scheduling.md) for live-schema checks, uploads, disclosure, link limitations and truthful completion states. A connected account is not a requirement to post every clip there.
