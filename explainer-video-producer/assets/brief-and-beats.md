## Opening and progression

Write the opening in spoken language: name the subject, connect it to a concrete need, promise a specific outcome, and orient the viewer to the route. Keep only the context this audience needs.

Outline the prerequisite order. Explain how each section resolves a question and leads to the next one. Identify the visible payoff and conclusion before drafting the full narration.

## Final review

Check the opening promise, orientation, prerequisite order, explanation-to-action transitions, real outputs, readable holds and closing payoff. Judge each scene by its teaching function. Document the actual final file, not only the planned composition.
